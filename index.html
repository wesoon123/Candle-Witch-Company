Candle care

Treat it right,
it'll treat you right.

A few habits keep every burn clean, even and safe — from the first light to the last.

i.

Burn it safely

Never leave a lit candle unattended or move it while it burns. Keep it on a stable, flat surface away from anything flammable — and out of reach of kids and pets.

ii.

Let the wax pool

On the first burn, let the wax melt all the way to the edge of the jar. That's what prevents tunneling for the life of the candle.

iii.

Not all day

For best performance, burn 3–4 hours at a time. Keep it away from drafts and windows — moving air causes fast, uneven burning.

iv.

Trim your wick

Trim before you relight to keep the wax clean. Flame too high? Snuff it, let it cool, trim. Just don't trim too short on the first burn.

A note on scent throw.

We use the amount of fragrance oil our suppliers recommend and work hard to make every candle as fragrant as it can be. Some scents are simply stronger than others, and soy doesn't throw quite as loud as petroleum-based wax.

We think a healthier, natural wax is worth that trade. Bigger room? Reach for a bigger candle — or light a couple.
